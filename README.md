# Amazon Store Sales Analysis 

An executive-level synthesis and analytics dashboard analyzing key performance metrics for an Amazon Store using Excel. This repository provides data-driven insights into regional customer distribution, channel performance, product category revenue, seasonal trends, and discount strategy efficiency.

---

##  Executive Summary

This project analyzes Excel sales dashboard data to answer critical business questions around declining YoY revenue, discount profitability, seasonal sales slumps, and geographic growth opportunities.

### Key Highlights
- **Revenue Distribution:** **73%** generated in 2022 vs. **27%** in 2023.
- **Sales Channels:** Desktop/Web leads at **55%**, while Mobile drives **45%**.
- **Top Categories:** **Beauty** ($124.4K) and **Home & Kitchen** ($112.2K) outshine Electronics ($93.6K).
- **Discount Impact:** High discount tiers led to **$54.1K in margin loss** while generating less revenue than low-discount strategies.

---

##  Key Performance Insights

### 1. Geographic & Channel Spread
- Customer demand is evenly distributed across five key regions: **North (22%)**, **West (21%)**, **East (20%)**, **Central (20%)**, and **South (17%)**.
- Mobile apps drive **45%** of overall revenue, highlighting the importance of mobile UI/UX optimization.

### 2. Product Category Performance
| Category | Revenue ($) | Performance Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beauty** | $124,444.45 |  Top Performer |
| **Home & Kitchen** | $112,189.22 |  Top Performer |
| **Books** | $108,456.07 |  Moderate |
| **Fashion** | $101,701.32 |  Moderate |
| **Electronics** | $93,628.55 |  Underperforming |

### 3. Discount Efficiency vs. Profitability
- **Low Discount:** $185,429.82 revenue with only **$8,919.67** total discount given across 160 orders.
- **Medium Discount:** $188,592.01 revenue with **$31,208.52** total discount given across 168 orders.
- **High Discount:** $166,407.78 revenue despite giving **$54,157.93** in total discounts across 172 orders.
>  **Takeaway:** Deep discounting significantly erodes margins without driving higher overall sales revenue.

##  Business Problems & Actionable Strategy

| Focus Area | Strategic Action | Expected Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discount Optimization** | Limit **High Discounts** to slow-moving clearance items; lean on **Low/Medium** discount tiers. | Protects gross margins while maintaining volume. |
| **Mobile UX Enhancement** | Optimize mobile checkout, push notifications, and offer mobile-exclusive deals. | Elevates mobile conversion closer to desktop levels (55%). |
| **Regional Marketing (South)** | Run localized campaigns and bundle Beauty + Home & Kitchen products. | Elevates South region share (17%) to match Northern benchmarks (22%). |
| **Mid-Year Revitalization** | Launch mid-year promotional campaigns during the June–October lull. | Flattens seasonal volatility between Q1 peaks and Q4 upticks. |
| **Electronics Audit** | Review pricing strategies, warranty offerings, and product reviews for Electronics. | Recovers category performance to align with non-tech sales. |
